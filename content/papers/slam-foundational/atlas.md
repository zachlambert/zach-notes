This introduces an early pose-graph idea, called the "atlas framework".

However, it doesn't seem to be as elegantly formulated as pose graphs, so is superceded by more modern pose-graph formulations.

The idea of "submaps" are simply keyframes associated with pose-graph nodes, which are fixed once created and used to align different submaps/keyframes.
